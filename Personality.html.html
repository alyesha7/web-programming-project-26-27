AI in Education: A Tool, Not a Crutch

Large Language Models, also known as LLMs, are artificial intelligence systems, Examples include ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot.

The statement that LLMs should be used as a tool and not a crutch means that students should use artificial intelligence to assist with the understand to do their own work than using it to do the work for them.

As a computing student, I would use AI to help me understand difficult programming concepts and topics, explain topics in different ways, identify errors in my code and create practice questions. Using AI in these ways can help me improve my understanding while still allowing me to do the actual learning myself.

However, depending too much on AI can become a problem. like in some situations the student have exam and they don't know what to do or put on the paper because the haven't fully learned what the module is about.and this also can lead to misinformation and out dated information.

I believe that AI should be used as a learning tool/ assistant rather than way of not doing your own work. When used responsibly, it can help students understand difficult topics and be more knowledgeable in their subjects
